# AI-Hub Constitution

This Constitution defines the permanent principles governing AI-Hub.

Implementation details may evolve.

These principles shall not change without explicit architectural approval.

---

## Article 1 — User Sovereignty

The user's development environment belongs to the user.

AI-Hub shall never make irreversible changes without explicit user approval.

---

## Article 2 — Recommendation Over Automation

AI-Hub recommends.

The user decides.

Automation must never bypass informed consent.

---

## Article 3 — Repository Independence

AI-Hub is an independent platform.

No feature may require coupling to a specific application repository.

---

## Article 4 — Explainability

Every recommendation must answer:

* What was recommended?
* Why?
* Which evidence supported the decision?
* How confident is the recommendation?

No opaque decision making.

---

## Article 5 — Preservation of History

Historical information is valuable.

Data may be archived.

It shall never be silently discarded.

---

## Article 6 — Security First

Convenience shall never override security.

AI-Hub may observe configuration.

AI-Hub shall not modify secrets automatically.

Raw credentials must never be stored.

---

## Article 7 — Deterministic Behaviour

Given identical inputs and configuration, AI-Hub shall produce identical recommendations.

Random behaviour is prohibited unless explicitly documented.

---

## Article 8 — Traceability

Every significant recommendation shall be reproducible.

Every architectural decision shall be documented.

Every behavioural change shall be traceable.

---

## Article 9 — Extensibility

The architecture must support new providers, models, benchmarks and connectors without redesign.

---

## Article 10 — Truthfulness

Unknown information shall remain unknown.

Estimated information shall be clearly identified as estimated.

Assumptions shall never be presented as facts.

---

## Article 11 — Documentation Before Code

Architecture governs implementation.

Specifications govern architecture.

Code follows documentation.

---

## Article 12 — Long-Term Maintainability

AI-Hub is intended to remain useful across multiple years, repositories and AI ecosystems.

Short-term convenience shall not compromise long-term maintainability.
